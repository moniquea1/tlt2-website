Our Approach

Not scores. Not types.
A language for love.

At TrueLuvTake2, we don't reduce people to scores, types, or algorithms. Instead, we reflect connection through five foundational conditions — relational qualities that help love take root, deepen, and endure.

Every person expresses all five — just in different ways and different seasons of life. This framework is not designed for comparison. It is designed for clarity.

The Framework

Five Elements, One Language

Each element reflects a distinct dimension of how connection forms, deepens, and sustains itself in relationship.

Earth
Ground & Capacity

The stability, readiness, and real-life availability that allow love to take root.

Reflects follow-through, steadiness, and the ability to make room for relationship in a full life.

Water
Emotional Flow

The movement of feeling, empathy, and repair.

Reflects how emotion is expressed, received, and navigated in connection — including the ability to repair.

Air
Pace & Boundaries

The rhythm, communication, and spaciousness that let love breathe.

Reflects how clarity is built through pacing, honest boundaries, and knowing your own needs.

Fire
Intimacy & Connection

The warmth, attraction, and aliveness that keep love vibrant.

Reflects chemistry, playfulness, and the spark that brings joy and desire into relationship.

Ether
Relational Support

The wider container around relationship.

Reflects the role of community, family, values, and broader life context in helping love grow sustainably.

The Three Levels

Not a Hierarchy —
Stages of Aliveness

Every element is reflected at one of three levels. These are not rankings — they're descriptions of where you are right now. All three are honored equally.

Rooted

Rooted

Well-established and actively expressed. This element is a current strength in how you approach connection.

Steady. Present. Real.
Growing

Growing

Present and developing. Something meaningful is here — and it is still taking clearer shape.

Forming. Alive. Moving.
Seeding

Seeding

Early, tender, and still forming. This element is not absent — it is quietly taking root.

Tender. Early. Possible.
How It Works

The Attunement Profile

Your responses move through six relational domains — one for each dimension of connection. There are no right answers. No scores are ever shown to you. What emerges is a gentle reflection — a portrait of how connection lives in you right now.

01

Complete the Profile

A quiet, reflective set of questions across six relational domains. About 10–15 minutes. Only honest answers — no right or wrong.

02

Scored Behind the Scenes

Weighted values work quietly in the background. You never see numbers or percentages — only the human language of your portrait.

03

Each Element Is Reflected

Earth, Water, Air, Fire, and Ether — each shown at Rooted, Growing, or Seeding, with a short narrative written for you personally.

04

You Receive Your Full Results

Five element reflections, your strengths, and what may want more care — returned as a private portrait, emailed directly to you.

05

Your Profile Shapes the Journey

Your profile becomes your relational portrait — the foundation your Advisory Circle draws from as they support your journey. They receive an overall impression of who you are and how you connect, giving them the context to advocate thoughtfully on your behalf.

Your private specifics remain yours alone. Your circle sees the portrait — not the details.
