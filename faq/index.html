The Process

How does it work?

What to expect when you step into TrueLuvTake2 — from your first reflection to your first introduction.

Your Attunement Profile is a reflective set of questions across six relational domains — designed to reveal how connection lives in you right now. It takes about 10–15 minutes and there are no right or wrong answers.

The results are reflected back to you through our Five Elements of Connection framework: Earth, Water, Air, Fire, and Ether — each shown at Rooted, Growing, or Seeding. You receive a private portrait. No scores are ever shown to you.

Our proprietary framework for understanding relational readiness — not a personality type or a compatibility score, but a reflective portrait of how love moves through you.

  • Earth — Ground & Capacity: stability, readiness, follow-through
  • Water — Emotional Flow: empathy, expression, repair
  • Air — Pace & Boundaries: rhythm, clarity, spaciousness
  • Fire — Intimacy & Connection: warmth, chemistry, aliveness
  • Ether — Relational Support: community, values, life context

Yes. Your full Attunement Profile is private — returned directly to you. Your specific results, reflections, and personal details are never shared without your explicit consent.

Your Love Team receives a high-level impression of your relational portrait — not your private specifics. The portrait, not the details.

You create your Introduction — a brief personal portrait including a photo and short video — which becomes your tile in The Garden. You then activate your Membership and invite your Love Team.

From that point, your Love Team does the searching. They browse the Garden, discuss candidates privately among themselves, and present their curated choices to you. You never scroll. You receive. That hand-off is the heart of the entire experience.

The Love Team

Questions about
the people beside you.

The Love Team is one of TrueLuvTake2's most distinctive features. Here's everything you need to know.

The Love Team is the trusted group of people you invite to search the Garden on your behalf — most often your adult children and a Trusted Love Advisor (TLA). They are the heart of what makes TrueLuvTake2 unlike anything else.

They browse. They discuss. They shortlist. They present. You never see a profile until your team believes it's worth your time.

Adult children will complete the Adult Child Profile — a thoughtful reflection that helps them understand their parent's relational portrait and what their parent is genuinely seeking in a partner.

They are then given access to the Garden, where they browse member profiles and discuss candidates privately with the other members of the Love Team. The Seeker never sees pictures, profiles, or introductions directly — that is entirely the Love Team's work. They search. They vet. They present. You decide.

Yes — the Love Team is a required and essential part of the TrueLuvTake2 journey. It is what makes this platform fundamentally different from anything else available.

Your Love Team — the people who love you and know you best — are the ones who browse the Garden and present curated introductions to you. You don't browse. You don't scroll. You receive. That hand-off is the product.

Your Love Team receives an overall impression of your relational portrait — enough context to search thoughtfully on your behalf. They see the portrait, not the details.

Your private specifics, scores, and reflections remain yours alone. The circle receives context, not confidences.
