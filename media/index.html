Topics worth talking about

Perfect for podcasts, panels, newsletters, and media appearances exploring midlife, relationships, aging, and modern connection.

01
Why Safety Matters More Later in Life

Romance scams have become an epidemic disproportionately targeting adults over 50, costing billions annually. Monique makes the case for why real identity verification and a trusted circle of advocates isn't just a feature — it's a fundamental act of respect for people who deserve better than the internet's status quo.

Read her thinking →
02
Why Geography Should Not Decide Your Future

Most dating platforms assume the right partner lives nearby. But for adults over 50 who have already built a full life, the question isn't convenience — it's alignment. Monique explores what opens up when you stop filtering by zip code and start filtering by values, readiness, and depth.

Read her thinking →
03
When Adult Children Become Guardians of Love

What happens when the people who know you best want to help you find love? Monique explores the beautiful and often untapped role adult children play in a parent's search for meaningful companionship — and why family isn't an obstacle, it's one of the most underused resources in modern dating.

Read her thinking →
04
Why We're More Intentional About Hiring Than Love

You wouldn't hire someone for an important role based on a photo and a two-minute conversation. Yet that's exactly how most adults approach finding a life partner. Monique challenges audiences to bring the same discernment they use in their careers to the most important decision of their personal lives.

Read her thinking →
05
40 Million Adults Over 50 Are Single. Almost No One Talks About It.

The data is staggering — and largely invisible in mainstream culture. Monique unpacks what it means that millions of emotionally available, experienced adults are being systematically underserved by every existing platform, and what changes when we finally take this population seriously.

06
Chemistry Isn't the Problem. Readiness Is.

The connections that felt electric but quietly fell apart weren't lacking chemistry — they were lacking readiness. Monique introduces a framework for emotional, relational, and practical readiness that is changing how adults over 50 think about entering a new chapter of love and companionship.

About the Founder

Meet Monique Alemán

Founder, TrueLuvTake2 · Boston, MA

Monique Alemán brings more than two decades of leadership experience across entrepreneurship, healthcare, and executive search. She has led national teams responsible for high-stakes professional matchmaking and multi-million-dollar revenue portfolios — giving her a unique perspective on discernment, alignment, and the human elements of successful long-term partnerships.

Her background includes ten years as a successful restaurant entrepreneur and multiple VP-level leadership roles within the healthcare industry. Her work is grounded in a lifelong study of human connection, emotional intelligence, and relational readiness — shaped by both professional expertise and transformative global travel, including immersive journeys in Egypt and Peru.

TrueLuvTake2 is the integration of everything she has learned — professionally, personally, and spiritually — about what it truly takes for love to take root later in life.
