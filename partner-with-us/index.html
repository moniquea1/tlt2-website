What Happens Next

Three ways your referrals
engage with the platform

Once connected with TrueLuvTake2, the people you refer can participate in three distinct ways — each designed for a different role in the journey toward love.

Always Available

The Attunement Profile

Where every journey begins. A quiet, reflective set of questions across five dimensions of connection. Results arrive privately by email — no scores shown, no comparison. Just an honest portrait of how love moves through someone right now.

Member Benefit

The Advisory Circle

For the people who love a Seeker most — adult children and trusted advisors who reflect separately on who their person is in relationship. Not to decide, but to advocate thoughtfully. Together, they form the Advisory Circle: the human context no algorithm can replicate.

When Ready

The Garden

The private member space where curated profiles are shared and interest is expressed with intention. Your referral earns their place here — reviewed, welcomed, and guided with care. No swiping. No algorithms. Just people, ready to be known.
