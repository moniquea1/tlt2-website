Company Overview

TrueLuvTake2 is a people-first platform designed to support meaningful connection later in life. Founded by Monique Alemán and launching in 2026, TrueLuvTake2 offers a human-guided alternative to traditional dating apps — built on principles of self-awareness, discernment, and real-world context.

Rather than replicating the speed, anonymity, and algorithm-driven mechanics of modern dating, TrueLuvTake2 introduces a model that mirrors how people make meaningful decisions in every other area of life. The platform centers readiness, authenticity, and introductions supported by trusted Love Teams — the people who know a Seeker best.

How It Works
Attunement Profile

A reflective profile assessing readiness across multiple dimensions. Shifts the question from "Who do you want?" to "Who are you now, and what are you ready for?"

Love Team

A personal love team of trusted people — typically adult children and a Trusted Love Advisor — who support and advocate for the Seeker throughout the process.

Authentic Presence

Every member submits a short video. No AI-generated profiles, no bots, no unverified identities. You will always know the person is real.

Curated Invitations

Potential connections are presented as thoughtful invitations — not endless profiles to scroll through. Intentional pacing at every step.

Five Elements Framework

A proprietary connection framework (Earth, Water, Air, Fire, Ether) replacing algorithms. Measures compatibility through lived experience, not data points.

The Garden

A private, members-only space where Love Teams browse profiles and signal interest on behalf of their Seeker. Browsing happens with support — never in isolation.

About the Founder

Monique Alemán

Founder & CEO, TrueLuvTake2

Monique Alemán brings more than two decades of leadership experience across entrepreneurship, healthcare, and executive search. She has led national teams responsible for high-stakes professional matchmaking and multi-million-dollar revenue portfolios, giving her a unique perspective on discernment, alignment, and the human elements of successful long-term partnerships.

Her background includes ten years as a successful restaurant entrepreneur and multiple VP-level leadership roles within the healthcare industry. Her work draws on a lifelong study of human connection, emotional intelligence, and relational readiness, shaped by personal journeys in Egypt and Peru centered on presence, reflection, and human connection.

Brand Guidelines
