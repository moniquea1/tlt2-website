TrueLuvTake2 Launches to Reimagine Love Later in Life: A Purpose-Led Alternative to Traditional Dating Apps

"Matching doesn't begin with data. It begins with self-knowing."

Monique Alemán, Founder — TrueLuvTake2

Dating apps are facing growing criticism for fostering superficial, transactional, and exhausting experiences. TrueLuvTake2, founded by Monique Alemán, is launching as a community-first platform designed to support meaningful connection later in life, grounded in self-knowledge, discernment, and real-world context.

A Shift Away from Swipe Culture

Rather than replicating the speed, anonymity, and algorithm-driven mechanics of traditional dating apps, TrueLuvTake2 introduces a more intentional model, one that mirrors how people make meaningful decisions in other areas of life.

"We invest extraordinary time, energy, and resources into hiring the right people professionally," said Alemán. "Yet when it comes to choosing a life partner, arguably the most important decision we make, we often rely on speed, instinct, and isolation."

TrueLuvTake2 was created to challenge that imbalance.

A People-First Model of Connection

"Ancient cultures understood that the most important unions require witnesses, wisdom, and time. TrueLuvTake2 is the first platform to bring that truth into the modern age."

Monique Alemán, Founder — TrueLuvTake2

TrueLuvTake2 replaces swiping and browsing with a supported, human-guided process:

  • Attunement Profile Members complete a reflective profile designed to assess readiness across multiple dimensions of life, shifting the focus from "Who do you want?" to "Who are you now, and what are you ready for?"
  • Authentic Presence A short video is required of every member to verify identity and ensure authenticity. In a landscape where romance scams and fake profiles disproportionately target adults over 50, TrueLuvTake2 takes a firm stance: no AI-generated profiles, no bots, no unverified identities. You will always know the person is real.
  • Love Team "Think of the Love Team as your love team," said Alemán. "These are the people who know you, care about you, and can see what you sometimes can't see in yourself. No professional matchmaker can replicate that." The circle typically includes one or two adult children plus a Trusted Love Advisor, with flexible configurations for those with one child or none. The Love Team is not optional. It is the foundation of the process.
  • Curated Invitations Potential connections are presented as thoughtful invitations, not endless profiles to scroll through.

Why It Matters Now

More than 40 million adults over 50 in the United States are single, many of whom are open to connection but disengaged from modern dating platforms. Conversations around loneliness, isolation, and dating fatigue are becoming increasingly visible across media and society.

TrueLuvTake2 is not another app. It is a different way of thinking about how connection begins.

"Love later in life is not rare," Alemán said. "It is simply under-supported."

More Than a Platform: A New Philosophy

While TrueLuvTake2 will evolve into a full technology platform, it is launching with a focus on community, conversation, and intentional engagement. Its philosophy is simple:

  • People-first, not algorithm-first
  • Discernment over speed
  • Context over anonymity
  • Connection supported by community, not isolation

"This stage of life is not a problem to solve," Alemán added. "It's a chapter to celebrate."

About the Founder

Monique Alemán is the Founder of TrueLuvTake2 and brings more than two decades of leadership experience across entrepreneurship, healthcare, and executive search. She has led national teams responsible for high-stakes professional matchmaking and multi-million-dollar revenue portfolios, giving her a unique perspective on discernment, alignment, and the human elements of successful long-term partnerships. Her background also includes ten years as a successful restaurant entrepreneur and multiple VP-level leadership roles within the healthcare industry.

Her work draws on a lifelong study of human connection, emotional intelligence, and relational readiness, shaped by both professional expertise and personal travel, including journeys in Egypt and Peru centered on presence, reflection, and human connection.
