# TrueLuvTake2 — Brand Terminology & Product Model

Single source of truth for language across the site. Use these terms exactly this way.

## Core terms

- **Introduction** — a member's *presence* in The Garden: their photo, video, and written essence. The equivalent of a "profile" on other dating apps — how a person shows up to be seen. (Members "create a free Introduction.")
- **Profile** — the **Attunement Profile**: a reflective assessment/survey across the Five Elements. What other apps would call a questionnaire. NOT a dating profile. Every member completes one.
- **Presentation** — the package a member's Love Team delivers when a vetted candidate is ready to meet them (a photo, a video, and a personal note on why they chose this person).

## The membership model

- Membership runs **two months**.
- In that window, the Love Team works to bring the member **up to three** vetted candidates.
- The member can **activate a third month at half price**, which brings **up to two more**, for **five presentations per cycle**.
- The goal is **efficiency, not lingering** — members move *through* TrueLuvTake2 toward real people, unlike traditional apps built around endless scrolling and swiping.
- By the time a candidate reaches a Seeker, the Love Team's vetting, interviews, and prequalification have already done the hard filtering — so Seekers meet people who have already passed many tests.

## The Love Team

- Up to **2 adult children** + **1 Trusted Love Advisor**.
- They browse The Garden on the member's behalf, discuss privately, and make Love-Team-to-Love-Team introductions (not cold contact between strangers).
