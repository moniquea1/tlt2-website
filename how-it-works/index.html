Your Six Steps

From self-knowledge
to your first introduction.

Every step was designed with intention. None of them ask you to compete, perform, or rush.

1
Free · Always

Take your Attunement Profile.

Before anything else, we ask you to look inward. The Attunement Profile is a quiet, reflective set of questions across six domains — it takes 10 to 15 minutes and returns something no dating app has ever offered: a personal readiness reflection delivered to your inbox.

  • No scoring, no comparison — only honest reflection
  • Results returned privately by email
  • Reflects each of the Five Elements at Rooted, Growing, or Seeding
  • Available to anyone — Seekers, your Love Team, and the curious
2
Free · Always

Create your Free Introduction.

Your Introduction is your presence in The Garden — the first glimpse others will receive of who you are. It is not a resume. It is not a profile optimized for clicks. It is a genuine, unhurried expression of yourself.

  • Upload your photo and video — your photo places your tile in The Garden
  • Write your essence — what matters to you, what you're ready for
  • Record a 60-second video — your video is what lets your Love Team step inside and begin searching on your behalf
  • Share your connection preference and location
  • Photo gets you into the Garden. Video opens the door for your love team to begin.
3
Free · Waitlist

Join The Garden and wait for the moment.

Once your Introduction is approved, you become a founding member of The Garden. Paid membership — and matching — activates when The Garden reaches 250 members. Until then, you are nurtured, kept informed, and part of a community that is building something real.

  • Receive curated updates, member spotlights, and founder reflections
  • Watch the founding counter grow toward 250
  • No payment required until the Garden opens
  • You are among the founding members — present from the day the Garden first opens
4
$444 to Activate

Activate your membership and name your Love Team.

When The Garden reaches 250 members, paid membership opens. Your $222/month commitment — with a 2-month minimum — activates your Love Team and begins the search on your behalf. You will never have to scroll, swipe, or stress.

  • Name up to 2 adult children and 1 Trusted Love Advisor
  • Each receives a personal invitation by email
  • Adult children complete their own profile
  • Your Trusted Love Advisor completes a brief 3-question reflection
  • Your Love Team does the searching so you don't have to
5
Active Membership

Your Love Team searches The Garden.

This is where TrueLuvTake2 becomes unlike anything else. The people who already love you — who know your history, your humor, your heartbreaks, and your hopes — browse The Garden on your behalf. They watch videos. They discuss. They reach out to other Love Teams and begin the process of real, human vetting.

  • Love Team browses member tiles: photo, video, essence, location
  • Your Love Team discusses candidates privately within the platform
  • Your Love Team reaches out to other Love Teams — Love Team-to-Love Team vetting, not cold contact
  • You are not scrolling. You are not stressed. You are living your life while your team works.
6
The Moment

Receive your presentations.

When your Love Team is ready, you receive a presentation — not a notification. Their photo, their video, and a personal note from the people who love you, explaining why they chose this person and what stood out. It should feel like opening something special on a very special occasion.

  • Up to 3 presentations in your first 2 months
  • Optional Month 3 at $111 — 2 more presentations (5 total per cycle)
  • You decide how and when to connect — text, call, video, in person — at your pace
  • "This could be my pathway to true love. And I don't have to search alone."
